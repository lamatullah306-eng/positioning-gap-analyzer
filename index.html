Positioning Gap Analyzer

Compare your brand with competitors on messaging, scorecard and price. Get metrics, white-space themes and prioritised moves.

Step 1

Brands and their messaging

Add your brand plus 2-6 competitors. Paste each brand's homepage / product page / ad copy (about 60+ words works best). Price is the typical entry price of a hero product.

Step 2

Scorecard

Score each brand 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent) on the factors that drive choice in this category. Set the weight (1-5) by how much customers care. Rename or add factors to fit your market.

Step 3 · optional

Customise keywords

The defaults suit consumer brands. Edit them to fit your category: add your own themes and complaint topics, or rename and remove existing ones. Reviews added in Step 1 feed the Voice of Customer analysis.
